TARNOVSKI: seventeen years of branding, led by Andrei Tarnovski

TARNOVSKI is a branding studio in Bucharest, on Calea Plevnei 145. In seventeen years we have built and rebuilt more than 120 brands for clients in 12 countries, from McDonalds and Audi to banks, wineries and international organisations. We work at studio scale, not factory scale: on every project what matters is who makes the decisions, not how many people sit in the meeting.

Why this studio exists

Over seventeen years we have watched plenty of companies with good products lose to clearer rivals. Almost never was the problem the quality of the drawing. The problem was that nobody had decided, concretely, who the company should be the obvious choice for. That is where our positioning comes from: most brand problems are not design problems, they are choice problems. We wrote a short manifesto about it, because the thesis deserves to be explained once, properly. It is also why we work at studio scale: the questions of choice have to be asked by someone who then stays in the project through implementation, not handed to a department that has forgotten why it asked.

How we work

No departments handing the project to each other. The first conversation happens with someone who decides in the studio, and the verdict is honest: if your problem is not a brand problem, we will tell you so and recommend you do not work with us, for now. If it is, we propose work on the part that is blocking you: brand strategy, visual identity, rebranding or packaging design. The first conversation does not end with pressure to sign; it ends with a verdict you can check yourself, using the questions on our service pages. The rest of the process detail lives there.

Who we work with

Companies with a good product and a brand left behind: B2B suppliers whose value does not show in the quote, manufacturers losing on the shelf, service companies that look the same as every competitor. We do not work with everyone who asks: if a project has no choice problem to solve, we decline, because we would have no honest way to solve it. If you are not sure which category you are in, that is already a sign; we will discuss it in the first thirty minutes.
