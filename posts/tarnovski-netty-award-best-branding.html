TARNOVSKI Branding Studio Wins Netty Award for Best Branding

2024-04-23

Award-Winning Brand Strategy Earns TARNOVSKI Branding Studio Prestigious Netty Award for “MITP: Europe's First e-Park for Innovative Tech Businesses”We are thrilled to announce that Tarnovski Branding Studio, a leading provider of brand strategy and development services, has been recognized for its exceptional work in the Best Branding (Europe) category at the Netty Awards. Often referred to as one of the most esteemed accolades in the digital age, the Netty Awards celebrate accomplishments across the digital landscape, honoring top companies and leaders in over 100 distinct categories. Winning a Netty Award sets a benchmark for excellence and is a testament to the creativity, innovation, and technical prowess demonstrated by the winners."We are incredibly honored to be recognized by the Netty Awards," said Andrei Tarnovski, Co-Founder of Tarnovski Branding Studio. "Our team has poured countless hours of work and creativity into the MITP Branding Project. This award validates their hard work and dedication, and I couldn't be more proud."Our winning entry, "MITP: Europe's First e-Park for Innovative Tech Businesses", showcases a groundbreaking approach to branding. Through in-depth market and consumer research, combined with strategic planning and development, we crafted a comprehensive brand strategy that perfectly captured the essence of Moldova Innovation Technology Park. This strategic foundation translated into outstanding results, establishing MITP as a leading voice within the IT industry.Netty Award recipients are meticulously chosen based on a number of factors, including creativity, technical proficiency, innovation, and overall excellence in their respective fields. This win serves as a powerful affirmation of the hard work, innovative thinking, and dedication that our team poured into this project.As we celebrate this achievement, we want to express our deepest gratitude to our incredible team whose talent and commitment made this win possible. We would also like to extend our thanks to our clients and customers for their unwavering support and trust in our work.
