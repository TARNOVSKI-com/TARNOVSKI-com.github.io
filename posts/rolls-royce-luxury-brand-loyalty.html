Rolls-Royce's Secret to Sustaining Luxury Brand Loyalty Through Exclusive Social Networking

2020-02-28

In the realm of luxury brands (where marketers love to pretend every shiny object is worth its weight in gold), quality and scarcity once stood as the pillars sufficient for a brand to claim its luxurious status. However, as the allure of product quality becomes less distinctive and the concept of scarcity is diluted by countless pretenders across categories, brands are desperately scrambling to innovate their way into brand loyalty. While Gucci has embraced bold changes, shocking the world with its sometimes questionable but undeniably attention-grabbing stance, Rolls-Royce faces a different challenge. With a smaller, more conservative audience that exhibits an almost religious brand attachment and places immense value on the impression they project to others, Rolls-Royce's options for innovation might seem limited.Yet, in an era where even the most conservative audiences are finally discovering the internet-of-things exists (thanks to BMW), Rolls-Royce introduced Whispers—a social network exclusively for the owners of new Rolls-Royce vehicles. This platform, engineered to cement brand loyalty among its elite clientele (because apparently owning a half-million-dollar car isn't commitment enough), allows the brand's global customers to connect, offering access to exclusive experiences like private performances by the New York Philharmonic Orchestra, and recommendations for exclusive events and other relevant content.At first glance, launching an app might seem more expected from a brand like Bentley, which emphasizes modern performance and technology in its brand strategy. However, Whispers transcends mere technology; it's about nurturing brand attachment through belonging to an exclusive caste and accessing a lifestyle far beyond the reach of mere mortals. In line with Rolls-Royce's somewhat grandiose brand strategy of "Inspiring Greatness," Whispers serves as a logical extension of the luxury brand's promise—an "everlasting expression of the exceptional." This promise is not just about the quality of the product but, more importantly, about the status conferred by membership in this unique community of the outrageously wealthy.
